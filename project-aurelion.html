01Project overview

A coaching website built around a single clear decision.

AURELION is a self-initiated concept project: a complete editorial website for a fictional business coach. The goal was to show how a premium service business can present itself with clarity and confidence — without relying on hype, countdown timers, or invented statistics.

The design uses a forest-green and ivory system, an editorial serif paired with a clean sans-serif, and generous whitespace to create a calm, trustworthy impression. Every section builds toward one quiet, repeated action: booking a strategy call.

This is a concept project. AURELION is a fictional brand created to demonstrate design and frontend capability. It was not built for a paying client, and no business results, revenue, or booking figures are claimed.
02Intended audience

Established founders weighing a considered decision.

The concept imagines visitors who are established service-business owners and founders — people comparing a small number of coaches and looking for signals of seriousness, judgement, and fit before reaching out.

  • Decision-led, not impulse-led. They read carefully and value substance over urgency.
  • Sensitive to credibility. The design has to feel considered, because the service is premium.
  • Looking for fit. They want to understand the approach before committing to a conversation.
03Design direction

Editorial, restrained, and confident.

The art direction draws from editorial print: a strong typographic hierarchy, margin notes with section indices, and layouts that use alignment and space rather than decoration. Colour is used sparingly — deep green for authority, ivory for breathing room, and a muted sage as a single accent.

  • Type system. A high-contrast editorial serif for headlines, a modern sans for everything else.
  • Palette. Forest green (#123B2A), ivory (#F7F5EF), and sage (#A7B6A2).
  • Restraint. Subtle hover states and gentle reveal transitions — nothing that competes with the message.
04Messaging & conversion objectives

One message, one obvious next step.

The copy is structured as a short argument: name the stage the founder is in, frame the problem honestly, present a clear coaching approach, then make the next step easy. The primary call to action — "Book a Strategy Call" — is repeated consistently so the path forward is never ambiguous.

  • Clarity over cleverness. Plain language that explains who the coaching is for.
  • Honest framing. The offer avoids guarantees and fabricated outcomes.
  • A single primary action. Secondary links support exploration without distracting from the goal.
05Key design decisions

Choices made on purpose.

  • An "engagement dossier" replaces a generic pricing table, presenting who it's for and what's covered as an editorial spec sheet.
  • A duotone portrait treatment keeps imagery on-brand and consistent, with a graceful monogram fallback if an image fails to load.
  • A three-stage method (Clarify, Strengthen, Scale) gives the approach a memorable, logical shape.
  • An accessible FAQ answers objections before the call, reducing friction on the path to inquiry.
06Desktop & mobile presentation

Designed for both, not shrunk to fit.

The layout was composed as a responsive system. On desktop it uses an asymmetric grid with a portrait panel; on mobile it reflows into a single, deliberate column where the headline, offer, and call to action remain the focus.

07Key interface details

The small things that make it feel finished.

  • Accessible navigation with a keyboard-friendly mobile menu, visible focus states, and ARIA attributes.
  • Animated accordion for the FAQ, built on a proper disclosure pattern.
  • Reveal-on-scroll transitions that respect prefers-reduced-motion.
  • No horizontal overflow and responsive typography using clamp().
  • Progressive enhancement — the page remains usable even if JavaScript fails to load.
Built with semantic HTML, maintainable CSS custom properties, and vanilla JavaScript — no frameworks or unnecessary dependencies, so it deploys cleanly to static hosting.
